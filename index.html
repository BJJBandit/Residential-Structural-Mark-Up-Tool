Start with a plan

Open an architectural PDF or image, or use a blank sheet. Your plan stays in this browser and is never uploaded.

  1. Work through the Review tab: geotechnical report and wind, then roof, walls, load paths, footings and bracing.
  2. Set the scale, or calibrate against a known dimension.
  3. Draw walls, beams, columns and footings. Loads transfer automatically where members meet.
  4. Size members in the panel, then export the marked-up PDF.
